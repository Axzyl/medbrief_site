### 2. Problem
**Purpose:** Prove the problem is real and painful

**Pain Points:**
1. **Volume overwhelm:** Personal injury firms receive hundreds to thousands of pages of medical records per case — handwritten notes, faxed documents, scanned reports from multiple providers
2. **Skills gap:** Lawyers aren't trained to interpret clinical data; physicians aren't trained to identify legally material facts
3. **Time delays:** Manual physician reports take weeks or months, delaying case evaluation and settlements
4. **AI limitations:** Generic AI tools hallucinate and lack the precision and source traceability required in legal/medical settings

**Who experiences this:**
- Personal injury law firms
- Insurance defense lawyers
- Medical reviewers preparing litigation reports

**Supporting stat:**
- Medical record review accounts for 20-40% of case preparation time
- Physician assessment reports can take 10-30 hours per complex case

---

### 3. Solution
**Purpose:** Show how MedBrief solves the problem

**Three Core Capabilities:**

1. **Medical-Grade Document Extraction**
   - Processes handwritten notes, scanned charts, diagnostic reports
   - Preserves medical terminology, temporal context, and relationships
   - Handles poor-quality faxes and mixed layouts

2. **Auditable, Verifiable Reports**
   - Every data point linked to exact source location
   - Confidence scores on all extracted information
   - Uncertain data flagged for review (never inferred)
   - Structured summaries with injury chronology and treatment history

3. **Case-Specific Legal Intelligence**
   - Conversational interface for exploring medical evidence
   - Evidence-based legal argument generation
   - Query the full record without manual re-review

**Key Differentiator:** Source-level traceability makes every output defensible in court.

### 6. Business Model
**Purpose:** Explain how money is made

**Revenue Model:** B2B SaaS with usage-based pricing

**Pricing Tiers:**
- **Pay-per-case:** ~$70-140 per case (100-200 pages)
- **Individual Annual:** ~$10,500/year (~20,000 pages, 100-150 cases)
- **Team Plans:** Shared quotas with 15-25% volume discounts
- **Enterprise:** ~$300,000/year (~1M pages) with dedicated support

**Who Pays:**
- Law firms (primary)
- Medical reviewers
- Insurance carriers (enterprise)

## DESIGN & UX REQUIREMENTS

- One-page scroll for main pitch
- Large headings, short text blocks
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Professional blue color palette (trust/technology feel)
- No hidden pitch sections behind navigation
- Optimized for skimming, not reading
- Mobile responsive

---

## CONTENT PRINCIPLES

- Every section answers: "Why should the judge care?"
- Prefer visuals over text
- Avoid jargon — "litigation-ready evidence" not "NLP-powered extraction pipeline"
- Emphasize traceability and verification (key differentiator)
- Show don't tell: use concrete numbers (600+ pages, 70 cases, 20-40% time savings)

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

A judge or potential customer should be able to:
- Understand what MedBrief does in 30 seconds
- Grasp the problem and solution in 2 minutes
- See evidence of traction and team credibility
- Find contact information instantly
